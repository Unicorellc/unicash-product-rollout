Aggregator Model — No Licensing Costs

UniCash launches under an existing aggregator's MSB/MTL licenses, eliminating ~$240K in upfront regulatory costs and skipping the 3–4 month licensing wait. Remittances goes live by Month 4 alongside cash advances. Trade-off: slightly lower margins (aggregator rev-share of 15–30 bps).

⚠

Health Insurance: Deferred to Year 2

Group health insurance is not included in Year 1. Typical cost would be $500–$700/employee/month ($48K–$67K/year for 8 people). This will be added once the company reaches sustained profitability or at the start of Year 2. Founders and early hires should plan for individual coverage or COBRA in the interim.

💡

No dedicated Compliance Officer needed in Year 1

The aggregator handles regulatory compliance, BSA/AML program management, and state reporting. A Compliance Analyst (hired M4) handles internal KYC reviews and aggregator reporting. Full Compliance Officer hires when UniCash applies for its own licenses in Year 2.

Remittances is now a Day 1 product

Key risk: Aggregator dependency

The aggregator model trades licensing risk for dependency risk. Mitigations: (1) negotiate favorable long-term contract, (2) maintain relationships with 2+ potential aggregators, (3) begin own MSB/MTL applications in Year 2 once cash flow supports it.
