Why Not the Full $7M?

The 3-Year Exit Plan raises $7M across 4 tranches. But $2M of that (Tranche 4) is Year 2 growth capital. This analysis isolates what you truly need for Year 1 alone — the minimum to reach product-market fit and early revenue traction.

Cash Advances is the engine

At $19.13 ARPU/month and 60% adoption, Cash Advances alone generates 55-63% of total Year 1 revenue. This is your #1 product regardless of raise size. Card Program provides stickiness and direct deposit lock-in. Remittances is the growth multiplier but can wait until Month 9 if capital is tight.

⚠

The $2.1M Floor scenario has zero margin for error

Any single risk event — licensing delay, banking partner issue, or slower-than-expected revenue — is potentially fatal at the Floor raise level. The Lean scenario ($3.2M) survives most individual risks but not compounding risks. The Recommended raise ($4.0M) is the minimum where you can absorb 2+ simultaneous setbacks.
